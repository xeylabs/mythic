## Why this exists

xeylabs ships in public, but that only stays honest if the lab keeps the
freedom to also license its own codebase commercially (dual licensing, open
core, managed services). Without a CLA, a single outside contribution would
lock parts of the codebase against that forever. This agreement keeps the
codebase unified and the project sustainable — the code stays open, the
business stays possible.

## Mechanics

- Signing happens implicitly on your first contribution: the pull request
  template contains a CLA checkbox. Checking it binds you to the terms above
  for that contribution (and subsequent ones).
- Corporate contributions: if your company needs its own CLA schedule or an
  entity-level agreement, open an issue first so we can sort it out before
  you submit code.
- Questions about licensing or commercial use: open a discussion or contact
  an [xeylabs](https://github.com/xeylabs) organization owner.
